Transmission 01 // The Legend

In the summer of 1986, a cabinet appeared in exactly eleven arcades across three continents. No distributor. No serial number. Just a marquee that read STARRAIDERS and a joystick worn smooth by the first brave hands.

Players whispered that the attract mode whispered back. That Level 13 didn't exist until someone believed hard enough to find it. The cabinet vanished in 1989 — but the signal, as any true pilot knows, never really stopped.

This page keeps the flame lit. High scores. Lore. Fan art. And for those who have earned it… something else.
